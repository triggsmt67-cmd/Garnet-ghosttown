# Garnet historical content and citation audit

Reviewed October 12, 2026. Scope: local website copy, building records, all 13 published CMS stories, the timeline’s dates and categories, grant project histories, historical image credits, and relevant visitor information. This is a research audit, not a certification that every biographical detail is proven. A source that supports one paragraph does not establish an entire story. No CMS records were changed, and nothing was deployed.

## Other pages and grant histories

- **History/About/home:** major town chronology has institutional references; the population peak should remain approximate. The 1912 fire is established; exact text and casualty claims need the original issue. Era labels are editorial navigation, not historical sources.
- **Explore/buildings:** Davey’s business and records are supported by [Montana Historical Society MC-128](https://mhs.libraryhost.com/repositories/2/resources/1370). Its scope and date headings differ: do not treat archive date span as the store’s opening/closing dates. NPS nomination supports building histories. Sierra/Forest Lode exact claim dates need the site brochure or claim filings. Avoid treating a modern trail name as proof of a historic date.
- **Stark grant:** [NPS Stark School record](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/95001165) confirms 1995 listing and significant year 1916. Construction in 1915 and opening in 1916 may describe separate events. Obtain GPA award records for the 2020 grant and school/community records for the rest of the narrative.
- **Bonner grant:** [History Center newsletter, December 2024, p. 11](https://www.bonnermilltownhistory.friendsof2rivers.org/images/resources/newsletters/Newsletter_December_2024-Reduced_size.pdf#page=11) confirms the 2024 award; distinguish award date from installation/completion. A recipient homepage alone is not a complete citation.
- **Engine 1356 grant:** [Friends of the 1356](https://www.missoula1356.org/) acknowledges Garnet-funded lighting work. Confirm exact award year and tender completion with the award file/report; a planned repair is not completed work.
- **Alberton grant:** [NPS 1997 weekly list](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/nationalregister/upload/weekly-list-1997-national-register-of-historic-places.pdf) confirms Railroad Depot listing January 13, 1997. Obtain GPA 2019 award/completion report for the three windows; depot history from its nomination/town archive.
- **DeBorgia grant:** [National Register nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/3e97e1cd-9877-41b6-a912-a3e1d6cc2b26) supports 1908 construction, 1910 fire survival and school use through 1956. The local “some sources say 1958” parenthesis needs its named source. Obtain GPA 2017 award and completion record for lights, and recipient records for later events.
- **Get Involved/Association:** recent projects, 27-building assessment, spring 2027 completion, donation stories and finances are committee-supplied organizational claims. BLM history cannot verify future completion or annual financial percentages. Request project contract/report, artifact accession records and GPA financial/board records.
- **Events/updates:** these are time-sensitive CMS notices, not historic evidence. Confirm each notice’s source and dates with its organizer. This audit does not certify future events or live road/fire status.
- **Photo citations:** University of Montana courtesy credits already exist, but no per-image catalog links appear in the page captions. Use filename accession identifiers (e.g. 72-0593) to request/locate exact UM catalog records, photographer, date and required credit. Do not infer image date from the story year. Committee photos likewise need photographer/permission records, separate from text references.

## Book citation and proposed credit

[BLM full book](https://www.blm.gov/sites/default/files/docs/2021-07/THE%20ROAD%20TO%20GARNET%27S%20GOLD%20%28002%29.pdf) · [Government Publishing Office copy](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-I53-PURL-gpo159395/pdf/GOVPUB-I53-PURL-gpo159395.pdf)

For genuinely adapted passages, use a restrained version of Option 2:

> Portions of this historical overview are adapted from *The Road to Garnet’s Gold: A Brief History of Montana’s Gold Rush, Featuring One of the Last Ghost Towns*, edited by Tammie Adams (Bureau of Land Management, 2021).

For independently written text only checked against the book, use “Historical background checked against…” instead. “Courtesy of” can imply a relationship/permission not established by finding an online source. Avoid “final ghost towns managed by BLM,” which is not established by the book’s title. A general credit does not replace exact pages, newspaper citations, interview credits or image credits.
